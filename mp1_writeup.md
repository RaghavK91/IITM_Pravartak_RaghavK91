One can come to many conclusions and deductions after completing this mini project. After a comparitive analysis of the 4 prompting strategies involved, now it is time to announce the winner!
In my view, the winner is Structured prompting. Because, it gives the maximum bang for the buck. For a relatively fewer cost than few shot prompting and Chain of thought prompting, it gives the maximum accuracy.
What surprised me is the performance of structured prompting. It worked better than few shot prompting (which I thought would emerge as winner). The accuracy of the former is a tad better than the latter. For the "Acme Corp is hiring a Senior Software Engi..." Few shot performed little awfully which was surprising.
For my Capstone, I would choose between Few shot prompting and structured prompting. If structural reliability has to be prioritised I would go for the structured prompting or if the data has consistent patterns and the data has to be classified, then few shot prompting is the natural choice.
I would wish to try the classification of job postings by giving more fuzzy training and test data (essentially more snippets). I would try to mask the company name (a classified institution like MI5!), provide a more generic job description with catchy words and mask the number of years, yet conveying what level of experience is required for the job, more subtely. That would be a perfect tie breaker for different prompting techniques.
